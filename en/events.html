20–26 July 2026

90th Peach Festival.

The town dresses up for a week of tradition, tastings, live music and a farmers' market. The peaches of Castel Gandolfo — intensely sweet, with yellow or white flesh — take the lead in desserts, cocktails and chefs' dishes.

Nine decades of history: the first edition was held in 1936. Today the festival draws tens of thousands of visitors on its busiest days. Free entry, stalls open from the afternoon until midnight.
