Events · Music

Summer 2026 on the lake — concerts, opera, jazz.

November 20265 minute readEditorial team

Now that the village has slipped back into the slow pace of November, it is worth looking back. Summer 2026 had a special flavour: in July the Pope once again lived in the Apostolic Palace, and around him a programme that brought music to the square, the church, the papal courtyard and even onto the water of the lake.

Late June: music and spirituality

The season opened on 27 June with “Sui passi della musica e della spiritualità”, eight free events over three weekends promoted by the town council with the Pontifical Parish of San Tommaso da Villanova and the support of the Lazio Region (Comune di Castel Gandolfo). The programme moved through the village's landmark spaces: Bernini's church with Caccini, Mozart, Verdi and Bizet, the crypt with Marco Lo Russo Rouge's solo accordion, then Piazza della Libertà with the guitar ensemble of the Frosinone Conservatory, a tribute to Fabrizio De André and Pergolesi's “La Serva Padrona”. It closed on 12 July with an opera gala in the square (Comune di Castel Gandolfo).

July: the Pope in the courtyard

That same 12 July Leo XIV led the first of three July Angelus prayers in Piazza della Libertà, followed by those of the 19th and 26th (Diocese of Albano). On the evening of Saturday 18 July the courtyard of the Apostolic Palace hosted the Concert in honour of the Holy Father offered by the Diocese of Albano: the Polacca from Paganini's Third Violin Concerto, with Marco Rogliano, and the free fantasy on Bellini's Norma written by Luis Bacalov for pianist Rossana Tomassi Golkar, with I Musici di Parma conducted by Pier Carlo Orizio (Vatican News). The orchestra numbered 55 players (Vatican City State). At the end the Pope spoke of a beauty that helps us to see and to lift our gaze towards heaven (Vatican News).

From 20 to 26 July the 90th Peach Festival moved each evening to a different district, from the lakeshore to Pavona, before reaching the historic centre with the blessing of the peaches and two concerts in the square (Castellinforma). We told its story in a dedicated article.

August: rock, jazz and opera

From 12 to 16 August the lakeshore hosted the Castel Gandolfo leg of the Rock Festival, the touring series that also stopped in Albano, Torvaianica, Ciampino and Rome (RomaToday). On Saturday 22 August Piazza della Libertà welcomed double bassist Enzo Pietropaoli and singer Eleonora Bianchini for “Castelli in Jazz for SLA”: around three hundred people, free entry and donations for research into amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (Il Mamilio). On 28 August, on the lakeshore, a Grand Opera Gala with tenor Aurelio Grimaldi and soprano Olena Kovalova ranged across opera arias, operetta and Neapolitan song (Castellinforma).

“It is the first time a Pope has taken part in the procession on the stretch of water.”
Corriere della Sera, 29 August 2026

The finale on the water

The moment that will be remembered came on 29 August. After Mass in the church of the Madonna del Lago, Leo XIV followed the procession to the shore and boarded the Castelli Romani Park boat with the statue of the Virgin, crossing Lake Albano (Holy See Press Office). The crossing, about twenty-five minutes, was accompanied by some fifteen boats; the procession on the lake began in 1954, in the Marian Year of Pius XII (Vatican News). Paul VI and John Paul II had taken part, but neither had crossed the water by boat (Corriere della Sera).

What to note for 2027

Next year's official calendars have not yet been published, but some dates follow tradition: the Peach Festival falls in July, the feast of the Madonna del Lago at the end of August. Meanwhile the music does not stop: the Vatican Museums' “Music in the Museums” series brings concerts to the Apostolic Palace too until 27 November (Turismo Roma).
