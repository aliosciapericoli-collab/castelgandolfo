Christmas at Castel Gandolfo — nativities, markets, lights.

November 20265 minute readEditorial team

December at Castel Gandolfo is not the big-city kind: no crowds, a few well-placed lights, a Baroque square that becomes the village's living room. This guide separates what is already confirmed for 2026 from what recent tradition suggests, while we wait for the town council's official programme. It is an ideal month for anyone looking for the village at its most everyday, between Mass in the parish, a walk on the empty lakeshore and a coffee in the square.

Already open: the Barberini Nativity

Since 12 November the Apostolic Palace has been showing “The Light of Christmas”, a seventeenth-century Nativity tapestry chosen by the Vatican Museums and the Directorate of the Pontifical Villas for the holidays (Vatican Museums). Urban VIII commissioned it from the Barberini workshop in 1635: woven between September 1635 and February 1636 “with gold, silk and wool”, to a design the inventories attribute to Pietro da Cortona, it was meant for the papal chapel, probably the Sistine, as the backing of the baldachin behind the altar, with six matching pendants; above, angels hold a scroll with the words “Gloria in excelsis Deo” (Vatican Museums). A perfect link with the village: Urban VIII is the pope who began the papal stays at Castel Gandolfo, as we told in Spring 1626.

The tapestry is included in the Papal Palace ticket and follows its opening hours (Vatican Museums). Before setting off, check the Palace's official calendar (Vatican Museums): on 7 December, for example, the Vatican Museums list the Castel Gandolfo museum complex as closing from 2:30 pm (Vatican Museums).

5 to 12 December: cinema and peace

The Hallelujah Film Festival returns for its second edition, promoted by The Artist Club Italia with support from the Ministry of Culture: a week of screenings, meetings and testimonies across the church of San Tommaso da Villanova, Borgo Laudato si', the Centro Mariapoli and the Paolo VI School. The theme is human dignity in the face of artificial intelligence, in the spirit of Leo XIV's encyclical Magnifica Humanitas; on Sunday 6 December Monica Guerritore will receive the Art and Peace Award (Messaggero Veneto).

Music in the crypt

The crypt of Bernini's church hosts three concerts at 7:30 pm: Sunday 6 December violin and piano, Friday 11 “Aspettando Clara!” for clarinet and piano, Sunday 13 pianist Costanza Principe with Beethoven, Schubert, Liszt and Schumann, paid entry and no booking (Castellinforma). A quiet way to spend Advent, beneath the church designed by Bernini, in a space few visitors know.

In the square: what to expect

The 2026 municipal calendar has not yet been published, but last year's gives an idea of the rhythm. On 8 December the lights were officially switched on with a greeting from the mayor, followed by a party in the square with a box for letters to Father Christmas, an unwitting tribute to the letter box of 1820; on the 14th a nativity exhibition opened in the “Marcello Costa” council chamber; on the 16th the Paolo VI Pontifical School sang carols in Piazza della Libertà; from 19 to 23 and 27 to 29 December wooden market stalls filled the square; on the 31st the New Year's Eve concert, and on 6 January the Befana for children (Comune di Castel Gandolfo).

Last Christmas's most spectacular nativity was not in the square but at Borgo Laudato si': seventeen large life-size clay figures by the Mexican master Tiburcio Soteno, from the ceramic tradition of Metepec, restored at the town hall by his son Israel and blessed on 20 December by Cardinal Fabio Baggio (Comune di Castel Gandolfo). We will soon know whether 2026 has a similar surprise in store.

Practical tips

Days are short: arrive in the morning for the Palace and the viewpoint, and stay until sunset, when the square lights up. For dinner, the fraschette of Ariccia are a few kilometres away. To arrive without a car and choose where to stay, see our How to get there and Where to stay pages. We will update the Events page as soon as the town council publishes the 2026 programme.
